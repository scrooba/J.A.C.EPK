The History

Engineered Euphoria

J.A.C. is a retro-futuristic blend of funk and electronic music heavily influenced by the precision and soul of Daft Punk.

Each track is a transmission drifting through space, pulling you deeper into a mechanical world where every system moves in unison. Controlled at its center by just a computer, the sound balances 1970s underground aesthetic with polished, late-night energy.
