Locked in the Starship

Locked in the Starship picks up where Planet 24 leaves off, placing you inside a drifting vessel cut off from the system. Time slows, loops repeat, and movement becomes the only constant. Pressure builds, escape disappears, and the line between control and freedom begins to blur. Each track is a transmission from inside the ship, where sound becomes survival and there’s no way out but to keep moving.

Planet 24

Planet 24 is a mechanical world built on rhythm, where everything is in perfect sync and every system moves in unison. It runs on engineered euphoria, controlled at its center by just a computer. Each track is a transmission, signals drifting through space and pulling you deeper until you’re part of the system, left questioning what’s real.
